Space Weather Monitor

Space Weather Monitor is a research web page developed by Serhii Ivanov in 2026. It is designed to explore, visualise, and analyse publicly available real-time space weather data, including solar wind plasma parameters, the interplanetary magnetic field (IMF), and geomagnetic indices.

Data sources and acknowledgements:
Solar wind plasma and interplanetary magnetic field (IMF) data: National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Space Weather Prediction Center (SWPC), Real-Time Solar Wind (RTSW) .
Geomagnetic Dst index: World Data Center for Geomagnetism, Kyoto (WDC Kyoto), Real-Time Dst Index .
Estimated planetary Kp index: NOAA SWPC, Planetary K-index . The estimated Kp values are derived from near-real-time ground-based magnetometer measurements and may differ from the final official Kp index.

References:
1. Sugiura, M. (1964). Hourly values of equatorial Dst for the IGY. Annals of the International Geophysical Year, 35, 945–948.

Space Weather Monitor is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially associated with NOAA, the National Weather Service (NWS), or WDC Kyoto. It is not an official space weather forecasting service.

Development status: Space Weather Monitor is currently under development. Its features, data sources, visualisations, and data processing methods may be modified, expanded, or corrected at any time.

Usage restriction: Space Weather Monitor is intended exclusively for non-commercial use. Commercial use of the application is prohibited under all circumstances.
